Singular Values and Integer Powersof Accretive Matrices

Consider any accretive matrix $A \in \mathbb R^{n \times n}$, which means for any $x \in \mathbb R^n$ we have $x^T A x>0$. Do we have a universal bound constant $c$ s.t. $$\sigma (A)^2 \le c \sigma (A^2)$$ ? What about for cubic? Fourth power...? This notes deals with the question.
