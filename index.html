About

I am an undergraduate student in Applied Mathematics at the School of Science and Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research interests lie in theoretical optimization and the mathematical foundations of machine learning. I am interested in the dynamics, stability, and convergence of stochastic and adaptive optimization algorithms, viewed through tools from dynamical systems and stochastic analysis.
