Research

My interests center on optimization and machine learning. I am particularly interested in how mathematical analysis can explain and improve the training behavior of modern learning systems.

Research Experience

Mathematical Background

I am building background in convex optimization, probability, statistics, linear algebra, stochastic analysis, and theoretical machine learning.

A regularized empirical-risk objective can be written as

\[\min_{\theta}\; \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \ell\bigl(f_{\theta}(x_i), y_i\bigr) + \lambda R(\theta).\]

The geometry of this objective and the dynamics of the chosen algorithm both play a central role in theory and practice.
